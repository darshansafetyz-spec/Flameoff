FAQ

Common Questions

How does a fire blanket actually work?
By covering a fire completely, the blanket blocks its oxygen supply. Without oxygen, combustion stops and the fire dies — entirely physical, no chemicals involved.
Can Flame Off be reused?
Yes. After use, inspect for damage. If intact, fold it back and re-store. For major fires, replacement is recommended for full safety assurance.
Is it safe for EV battery fires?
Yes — our large-format blankets are suited for EV thermal runaway containment. They reduce heat spread and allow safe water cooling alongside suppression.
What sizes are available?
1m×2m (Standard), 2m×2m (Industrial), 1m×3m (Kitchen) and fully custom dimensions for B2B. WhatsApp us to discuss custom specifications.
How fast can I deploy it?
Under 5 seconds. Our single-pull tab design lets anyone deploy under full panic — no training, no nozzles. Works when you need it most.
Do you handle bulk B2B orders?
Absolutely. We supply distributors, fire safety firms, factories, and government bodies at wholesale pricing. WhatsApp your quantity for a quote within hours.
